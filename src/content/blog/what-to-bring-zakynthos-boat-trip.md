Knowing what to bring on a Zakynthos boat trip makes the difference between a relaxed day and repeated searches through an overfilled bag. You need protection from sun and spray, enough water, the correct document and a few practical items for swimming and photos.

This guide separates personal essentials from equipment that may already be on your boat. It also covers children, non-swimmers, motion sensitivity and marina arrival. If you are still choosing your trip, compare a [license-free boat rental](/license-free-rent) with a [boat rental with a skipper](/rent-with-skipper) before packing.

> **Key Takeaways**
> - Bring the document confirmed at booking, high-SPF sunscreen, water, secure sun protection and a dry phone pouch.
> - Pack light, use soft bags and confirm children’s safety gear before arrival.
> - Check your chosen boat’s exact inclusions rather than assuming food, snorkelling gear or transfers are provided.

## What to Bring on a Zakynthos Boat Trip in 60 Seconds

For either a 4-hour or 7-hour rental, start with the same core kit: confirmed identification, sun protection, swimwear, water, a quick-dry towel, secure footwear and phone protection. Add more drinks, snacks and personal essentials for the longer option, while keeping bags compact and easy to stow.

- Valid ID or passport, according to your booking confirmation
- High-SPF sunscreen and lip protection
- Secure hat, UV sunglasses and a light cover-up
- Swimwear and a quick-dry towel
- Water and simple snacks
- Waterproof phone pouch or small dry bag
- Charged phone, charging cable and power bank
- Personal medication you normally need
- Footwear with good grip
- Child-specific items confirmed with the operator

Before closing your bag, check the expected conditions. Our guide to [morning or afternoon boat rentals in Zakynthos](/blog/morning-or-afternoon-boat-rental-zakynthos) explains how timing affects comfort.

## Which Documents Should You Confirm Before Departure?

Bring the valid ID or passport specified in your booking confirmation. Requirements can vary by operator, named driver and boat, so your confirmation should take priority over a general checklist.

Keep the document inside a waterproof pouch rather than loose in a beach bag. Save your booking confirmation and operator contact details on your phone.

If you are hiring a self-drive boat, check [whether you need a boat licence in Zakynthos](/blog/no-license-boat-rental-zakynthos).

## Sun, Heat and Medication

Pack three forms of protection: high-SPF sunscreen, wearable shade and drinking water. A boat’s canopy can provide useful shade, but reflected light and time spent swimming still leave skin exposed. Apply sunscreen before departure and follow the product’s instructions for reapplication.

Choose a hat that fits securely, not one that lifts in the wind. UV-protective sunglasses are more useful with a strap, while a light shirt or cover-up protects shoulders between swims.

Bring necessary personal medication in its original packaging and protect it from heat and water. If you are concerned about motion sickness, speak with a pharmacist or doctor before the trip and follow the medicine label. Do not wait until departure to ask about dosing.

## Swimming Gear, Towels and Footwear

Wear or pack swimwear, one quick-dry towel per person and light clothing for the journey back. Quick-dry fabrics take less space than thick beach towels and are easier to manage after several swim stops. Footwear should grip well and stay secure when surfaces are damp.

Water shoes can help at rocky stops. Avoid loose flip-flops while boarding, and keep one dry layer for the return.

Do not assume masks, snorkels or fins are included. Ask your chosen boat or operator if you want to snorkel, then bring your own equipment if they confirm it is not supplied.

## Food, Water and Coolbox Planning

Bring enough water for every person and increase the amount for a 7-hour rental, hot weather or children. Simple snacks travel better than meals with sauces, fragile packaging or lots of waste. Confirm whether the coolbox is supplied and what you need to load into it.

Sota self-drive boats include a coolbox with ice, but food and drinks are not included. Sandwiches, fruit and sealed snack portions are easy to share and store.

Check any operator rules before bringing glass or bulky food containers. For Sota self-drive rentals, fuel is charged separately; check your booking confirmation before planning the day’s budget. ## Phone Protection and Charging

Use two layers of preparation: charge your phone fully, then keep it in a waterproof pouch or dry bag. A 7-hour day can drain a battery through navigation, photos and video, so a charged power bank and short cable are sensible additions.

Test the pouch before the trip and use a secure strap if you plan to photograph from the boat. Salt spray can reach a phone even when it never falls into the water. A soft lens cloth helps with spray marks without scratching the camera glass.

Download tickets, booking messages and maps before leaving your accommodation.

## Family, Child and Non-Swimmer Essentials

Families need the same essentials plus familiar snacks, spare dry clothing and sun protection that children will keep on. Before arrival, tell the operator each child’s age and approximate size, then confirm that suitable safety equipment is available for your group and chosen boat.

Non-swimmers should also be mentioned during booking and again at the safety briefing. Follow the operator’s instructions about safety equipment and remain within the limits explained for the day. A flotation aid does not replace active adult supervision or make an unsuitable swimming stop safe.

See the [best Zakynthos boat route for families with children](/blog/best-family-boat-route-zakynthos). A skipper may help when children need close supervision.

## What Sota Provides — and What to Confirm

The self-drive boat details list six useful onboard features: a coolbox with ice, Bluetooth speaker, sun canopy, sunbathing area, GPS tracking and safety equipment. Exact inclusions vary by boat, so treat your booking confirmation as the final list rather than assuming every model has identical equipment.

Confirm these points before packing:

- Safety equipment suitable for every passenger, including children
- Whether snorkelling equipment is supplied
- Any food, drink or container restrictions
- Storage space and any baggage guidance
- Toilet or shower availability on your chosen boat
- Transfer arrangements to and from Zakynthos Marina
- Fuel terms, payment details and any insurance questions

Food, drinks and transfers should not be assumed. Nor should you assume a toilet, shower or snorkelling equipment is available unless your chosen boat’s details say so.

## What to Leave Behind

Leave behind anything bulky, fragile or difficult to protect from spray. A compact soft bag is more practical than hard luggage. Pack for the trip you booked, not every possible situation.

Do not bring equipment based only on another company’s listing or an old social post. Boat layouts and inclusions differ. If an item matters to your comfort, check the [Zakynthos boat rental FAQ](/faq) before deciding whether to carry it.

## Night-Before and Marina Checklist

The night before, complete a 10-minute check: charge devices, chill drinks, protect your identification and review the booking message. Confirm the departure time, duration and exact meeting point.

**Night before**

- Check the weather update and operator messages
- Charge your phone and power bank
- Prepare water, snacks and personal medication
- Pack ID or passport and booking details
- Lay out swimwear, cover-up, hat and grippy footwear

**On arrival**

- For a Sota booking, meet at Zakynthos Marina using the pin and time in your confirmation; other operators may depart elsewhere
- Allow the arrival time stated by the operator
- Declare children, non-swimmers and any safety-equipment needs
- Listen to the full briefing before loading loose items
- Ask any final questions about fuel, route limits or onboard equipment

## FAQ

### Do I need to bring my passport on a Zakynthos boat trip?

Bring the valid ID or passport requested in your booking confirmation. Requirements can depend on the operator, booking and named driver, so keep the specified document protected from water and accessible at check-in.

### Are towels provided on the boat?

Do not assume towels are supplied. Bring a compact quick-dry towel for each person unless your chosen boat or operator confirms otherwise. It takes little space, dries between stops and gives you a dry layer for the return journey.

### Do I need to bring food and water?

Yes, bring your own water and simple snacks unless your booking explicitly includes them. Self-drive boats include a coolbox with ice, with exact inclusions varying by boat, but food and drinks are not part of that standard list.

### Should I bring snorkelling equipment?

Check your chosen boat or operator before packing it. Snorkelling equipment should not be assumed. If it is not included and swimming below the surface matters to your plans, bring equipment that fits you and store it in a compact bag.

### What should I bring if I get motion sickness?

Speak with a pharmacist or doctor before travelling, then follow the medicine label. Pack any necessary personal medication securely and keep it accessible. You may also prefer a morning departure, but conditions vary and the operator’s advice on the day remains important.
