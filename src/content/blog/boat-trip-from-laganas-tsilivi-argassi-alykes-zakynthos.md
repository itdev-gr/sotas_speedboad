Most Zakynthos visitors do not sleep at the marina. They sleep in Laganas, Tsilivi, Argassi or Alykes — then ask the same question: which port should we actually leave from for a boat day?

The answer is not always “the closest beach bar with a ticket desk.” The right departure point depends on the coast you want, whether you are booking a private rental or a shared excursion, and how much of your day you are willing to spend in a taxi before you even start the engine.

Sota Travel operates from **Zakynthos Marina** on the east coast. This guide explains how that base fits resort guests from the main hotel zones, and when another harbour logic makes more sense. For harbour-by-harbour route geography, use the companion [departure points guide](/blog/where-to-rent-boat-zakynthos-departure-guide).

> **Key takeaways**
>
> - Choose the coast first, then the port — not the reverse.
> - Laganas and Argassi guests often favour south-coast plans; Tsilivi and Alykes guests often favour easier east-coast access.
> - Zakynthos Marina is a practical central base when you want one operator for south or north options.
> - Transfer time is part of the real trip length. Count it before you compare “4-hour” offers.

## Quick Answer by Resort Area

| Staying in | Best first question | Typical fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Laganas** | South coast or Shipwreck day? | South private boat / Turtle Island–Keri plans are usually the cleanest fit |
| **Tsilivi** | Private rental or shared east/north tour? | Easy access toward Zakynthos Marina and east-coast operators |
| **Argassi** | How much transfer am I willing to do? | Short hop to town/marina; south or flexible east-coast plans work well |
| **Alykes / Alykanas** | Local east-coast cruise or longer transfer south/west? | East-coast convenience is high; west-coast Shipwreck days need more logistics |

These are planning defaults, not hard rules. Sea conditions, boat type and your group’s priorities still decide the final booking.

## Laganas: Closest Mentally to the South Coast

Laganas guests naturally think about turtles, Marathonisi and the south. That instinct is usually right.

A south-coast private day — Turtle Island, Keri Caves, Mizithres and a swim stop — keeps travel time productive. The [Keri Caves and Turtle Island tour page](/keri-caves-turtle-island-boat-tour) and the [south route blog](/blog/turtle-island-keri-caves-mizithres-rocks-boat-zakynthos) explain what that day includes.

If your Laganas group wants Navagio, understand the trade-off: you are choosing a north/west landmark day, not a “nearest beach” day. Porto Vromi operators market short west-coast crossings for a reason. From the south resorts, that means a longer land transfer before a shorter sea leg. For many private renters, a skippered plan from a central or southern base with a realistic route is cleaner than chasing the shortest possible Shipwreck crossing at any logistics cost.

**Practical Laganas tip:** Decide south vs Shipwreck before you compare prices. They are different products.

## Tsilivi: Easy East-Coast Access

Tsilivi sits on the northeast coast and is one of the most convenient resort bases for reaching east-coast departure points, including Zakynthos Marina.

That makes Tsilivi a strong base for:

- [License-free self-drive rentals](/license-free-rent)
- [Private skipper cruises](/rent-with-skipper)
- Flexible days that can lean south or north depending on conditions

Tsilivi guests sometimes assume they need a boat that leaves from the resort strip itself. Local options exist, but a short transfer to the right harbour often buys a better boat, clearer briefing and a route that matches what you actually want to see.

**Practical Tsilivi tip:** Ask for total door-to-boat time, not only sailing hours.

## Argassi: Short Transfer, Flexible Choices

Argassi is close to Zakynthos Town and the marina belt. For many guests, that is the quiet advantage: less transfer friction than the far south or far north resorts.

From Argassi you can realistically compare:

- A south-coast wildlife and caves day
- An east-coast scenic cruise
- A longer private itinerary if sea state and duration allow

Because the transfer is short, Argassi is also a good base for morning slots. You can leave the hotel later than a west-coast day-tripper and still catch calmer water. Pair this with the [morning or afternoon rental guide](/blog/morning-or-afternoon-boat-rental-zakynthos).

## Alykes and Alykanas: Convenience First, Then Ambition

Alykes and nearby Alykanas reward guests who value a calmer resort rhythm. East-coast boat logistics are usually straightforward. Ambitious west-coast plans are not impossible, but they ask more of the day: longer transfers, tighter timing and more weather sensitivity.

For families and mixed groups staying here, a private boat day with a clear coastal cluster beats a packed island fantasy. If your group is large, confirm capacity early using the [8–12 people boat guide](/blog/private-boat-8-12-people-zakynthos).

## Transfers, Taxis and Hidden Time Cost

A “cheap” boat that leaves from the wrong coast can cost more in taxis and lost swimming time than a clearer private plan from a slightly farther harbour.

Before you book, ask:

- Exact meeting point, not only the resort name
- Whether parking or taxi drop-off is simple
- Whether hotel pickup is included, paid extra, or not offered
- How delayed transfers affect your slot

Count transfer + briefing + sailing + swim stops as the real day. The brochure duration is only the boat portion.

## FAQ

**Can I do a boat trip if I am staying in Laganas?** Yes. Laganas guests are well placed for south-coast private boat days. If Shipwreck is the priority, plan the land transfer and harbour choice deliberately rather than assuming the nearest sales desk is the best route.

**Is Tsilivi a good base for renting a boat in Zakynthos?** Yes. Tsilivi has convenient access to east-coast departure points, including Zakynthos Marina, which makes private self-drive and skippered options easy to reach.

**Do I need a hotel transfer for a boat rental from Argassi?** Often a short taxi is enough because Argassi sits close to town and the marina area. Confirm the exact meeting point when you book.

**What about Alykes or Alykanas?** Both work well for east-coast logistics. Keep west-coast ambitions honest: the transfer and sea exposure change the shape of the day.

**Is Zakynthos Marina better than renting from my resort beach?** “Better” depends on the boat, briefing, route permissions and coast you want. A short transfer to the right operator often beats a weak local option that cannot run the itinerary you have in mind.
