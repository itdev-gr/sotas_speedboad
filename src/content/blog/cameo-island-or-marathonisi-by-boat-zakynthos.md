Boat day planners often lump **Cameo Island** and **Marathonisi (Turtle Island)** into one checkbox: “turtle bay south.” Operators hear it daily. The stops are close in geography but different in access, atmosphere, Marine Park sensitivity and what your group should expect from a swim.

This article compares the two for boat guests only. It does not replace the full [south Zakynthos route guide](/blog/turtle-island-keri-caves-mizithres-rocks-boat-zakynthos) or the [Marine Park rules](/blog/zakynthos-marine-park-boat-rules). It helps you pick the stop that matches your brief.

> **Key takeaways**
>
> - Marathonisi is the classic Turtle Island nature stop with strict distance rules.
> - Cameo is a connected islet experience with a different access and beach-club feel.
> - Neither guarantees a turtle encounter; both require respectful behaviour in park waters.
> - Most strong south days include Keri Caves and Mizithres, not only one islet.

## Quick Answer: Cameo or Marathonisi?

Choose **Marathonisi** if you want the recognised Turtle Island shape, nature-first framing and a stop that fits the standard [Keri Caves and Turtle Island tour](/keri-caves-turtle-island-boat-tour) narrative.

Choose **Cameo** if your group wants the islet-linked beach experience and you understand it is a busier, more “scene” environment than a quiet nature landing.

If you only have time for one islet emphasis, pick based on **crowd tolerance** and **swim style**, not based on which name sounded more exotic in a hotel brochure.

## Marathonisi (Turtle Island): What the Boat Stop Is

Marathonisi is the crescent islet in Laganas Bay associated with loggerhead turtle nesting habitat. From the water it delivers the postcard silhouette people mean when they say “Turtle Island.”

**Typical boat experience:**

- Approach by permitted routes with briefing on speed and distance
- Swim from the boat in designated patterns, not a free-for-all beach takeover
- Strong chance of clear shallow water on calm mornings
- Wildlife sightings possible, never promised

**Best for:** Families who want the nature story, first-time south-coast visitors, snorkelers who prefer calmer entries when conditions allow ([snorkeling by boat](/blog/snorkeling-by-boat-zakynthos)).

**Watch for:** Marine Park compliance, midday traffic, and the gap between “seeing turtles on Instagram” and respectful distance in real life.

## Cameo Island: What the Boat Stop Is

Cameo is the small islet linked to the mainland by a narrow strip, with a well-known beach-club atmosphere on busy days. From a boat, guests often experience it as a swim-and-social stop rather than a remote nature reserve landing.

**Typical boat experience:**

- Photo-friendly turquoise shallows when the sea is settled
- Higher energy and music carry from the shore scene in peak weeks
- Less “remote island” fantasy, more “south coast highlight reel”

**Best for:** Groups who want lively water, easy swimming photos, and do not mind shared-space energy.

**Watch for:** Expectations mismatch if you booked a quiet nature day but arrive at peak party density.

## Side-by-Side Comparison

| Factor | Marathonisi (Turtle Island) | Cameo Island |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary vibe | Nature and Marine Park story | Iconic islet swim, social energy |
| Turtle context | Central to the stop’s meaning | Possible nearby, not the focus |
| Crowd feel | Traffic, but nature framing | Often busier scene in peak season |
| Fit for young kids | Strong on calm mornings | Depends on tolerance for noise |
| Photography | Classic island silhouette | Strip-of-sand aerial favourite |
| Rules sensitivity | High: distances and speeds | Still park waters: stay briefed |

## How Each Stop Fits a Full South Day

Neither islet should usually be the **only** plan. The south coast wins when you chain short legs:

1. Marina departure (morning preferred)
2. Marathonisi **or** Cameo emphasis, based on the table above
3. Keri Caves for shelter and colour
4. Mizithres viewpoint and final swim if sea state allows

That shape is why private days for couples and large groups keep returning to the same geography with different emphasis. See [couples private boat day](/blog/private-boat-tour-couples-zakynthos) and [8–12 people guide](/blog/private-boat-8-12-people-zakynthos).

## Marine Park Rules Apply to Both

Loggerhead habitat does not switch off because a stop looks commercial or wild. That means:

- No chasing turtles for photos
- No blocking surfacing animals
- Follow skipper or briefing instructions on speeds and zones
- Do not assume every swim zone is open every hour

Full rule context: [Zakynthos Marine Park boat rules](/blog/zakynthos-marine-park-boat-rules).

## Self-Drive, Skipper or Group Tour?

**Skippered private** makes sense when you want the captain to choose Marathonisi versus Cameo based on today’s wind, traffic and your group’s mood.

**Self-drive** can visit south stops within permitted areas if your briefing and hull allow it. Review [license-free rentals](/license-free-rent) and [self-drive vs skipper](/blog/self-drive-or-skipper-boat-rental-zakynthos).

**Group tours** often tick one islet quickly alongside other landmarks. Fine for a first taste; weak if you wanted an unhurried swim club.

## FAQ

**Which is better, Cameo Island or Marathonisi?** Neither is universally better. Marathonisi fits nature-first and classic Turtle Island expectations. Cameo fits groups who want the famous islet swim and accept a livelier shore atmosphere.

**Can you see turtles at Cameo or Marathonisi?** You may see loggerhead turtles in park waters, especially around Laganas Bay, but sightings are never guaranteed and approaching them is not allowed.

**Do boat tours go to both in one day?** Some itineraries emphasise one islet plus Keri and Mizithres rather than both islets in depth. Longer private days can include more stops if conditions and time allow ([4-hour vs 7-hour rental](/blog/4-hour-vs-7-hour-boat-rental-zakynthos)).

**Is Cameo the same as Turtle Island?** No. Marathonisi is the stop most closely associated with the Turtle Island name. Cameo is a separate islet experience with its own access character.

**Which stop is calmer for seasickness-prone guests?** On a settled morning, both can work as short swim legs on a south plan. The bigger comfort lever is overall route exposure, not the islet name alone ([seasickness guide](/blog/seasickness-boat-trip-zakynthos)).
