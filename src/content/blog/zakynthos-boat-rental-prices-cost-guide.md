## Choosing Between a 4-Hour and 7-Hour Rental

A 7-hour reservation supplies three extra hours, or 75% more booked interval, than a 4-hour choice. Its cost does not automatically rise by 75%, nor is the longer period inherently better value. Check both live amounts for the same model before judging the difference.

## How Fuel Is Calculated

Fuel is excluded from Sota Travel's self-drive base fee and charged according to actual use. Moving from four to seven hours does not create a fixed petrol surcharge. Distance travelled, vessel choice and the outing's pace influence consumption, making any advance guess misleading.

Extra time spent anchored may burn little additional petrol. Conversely, a shorter voyage covering greater distance can consume more. Sea conditions, passenger load and operating speed also affect usage.

Before departure, ask how the operator records consumption, when the bill is confirmed and which settlement method applies. This component stays variable and outside the displayed self-drive fee. Generic online estimates or yacht-marketplace averages cannot replace booking-specific answers.

## A Fair Quote-Comparison Method

Sort every proposal into three buckets: base hire, known extras and variable costs settled later. Sota Travel publishes boat-specific 4-hour and 7-hour self-drive figures with petrol separate. Apply this framework to any July 2026 listing, even when its headline presents charges differently.

Use this sequence:

1. Match equal timespans, or establish what each selection provides within its allotted time.
2. Verify the passenger limit, then calculate per-person cost only when your entire group fits.
3. Identify whether the product is private self-drive, private with captain or a shared tour.
4. Place bundled equipment and services beside the starting amount.
5. Keep fixed add-ons apart from variable petrol instead of forcing a false total.
6. Consider launch points and the expense of reaching them.
7. Seek written clarification whenever “from”, “all inclusive” or “fuel extra” remains ambiguous.

That process exposes false bargains without dismissing better equipment or a convenient departure because its headline is slightly higher.

### Is €180 the final price?

€180 is a starting point, not a universal final total. Vessel and duration determine the base hire; petrol remains variable and billed according to use. Avoid inventing a fixed number.

### Does a 7-hour rental always offer better value?

No. Seven hours supplies three additional hours, but value depends on the rate difference, route and your plans. Examine both choices, then select the shorter window when extra time adds nothing.

### Is fuel included in Zakynthos boat rental prices?

That depends on the listing. For Sota Travel's self-drive fleet, petrol is separate and based on use. Ask how consumption is measured and settled before weighing it against a fuel-inclusive offer.

### How much does a private cruise with a skipper cost?

No universal skipper rate exists. The amount depends on vessel, itinerary and duration. Review private options, then [contact the team](/contact) instead of attaching a guessed supplement.

### Should I compare prices per boat or per person?

Begin with the complete vessel amount. Divide by your actual party size only when everyone fits within stated capacity. Per-person maths should never conceal differences in duration, privacy, captain service, petrol or inclusions.
